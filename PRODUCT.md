# Docket
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Delegated by the user. Python stdlib and SQLite for a private local record desk; a pinned JavaScript Attestcoin SDK adapter and Solidity registry for public inclusion verification. Static web UI with self-hosted Public Sans.
## Users
Small teams and grant reviewers checking crypto payment records across chains.
## Product Purpose
Match a private invoice to a source transaction, keep clear review notes, and export the evidence behind each decision.
## Capabilities and Constraints
User-selected scope: one source chain (Ethereum Sepolia), salted invoice commitments, duplicate detection, source transaction/receipt inspection, Attestcoin inclusion proof checked on CC3, and a registry binding the invoice and transaction to its submitter. Inclusion is never labelled payment. Receipt success and payment matching are separately labelled RPC checks. No payout or credit scoring. Invoice contents and salts remain local unless explicitly exported. Public testnet deployment is not yet verified.
## Brand Commitments
Simple name, professional task-focused interface, plain explanatory English, no em dashes, hype or generic AI decoration. Stack and design decisions delegated. Captions-only demo with real behavior.
## Evidence on Hand
SELECTED-IDEA.json is the exact approved scope. No user interviews or customer impact claims exist.
